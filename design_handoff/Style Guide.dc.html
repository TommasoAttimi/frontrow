Logo & Mark

The wristband is our core symbol — representing admission, proof of presence, and the physical moment of being at a concert.

Primary Logo — Option B (Dark + Orange)

Wristband Icon

Dark rounded square (#111118) with orange wristband (#e8752a) running through the centre. Perforation line, "FR" stub text, and check icon on the right. Reads clearly at all sizes.

Alternative Logo — Option C (Inverted)

Inverted Wristband

Muted orange background (#e8752a @ 92% opacity) with darker wristband (#2a2420). Use on darker page backgrounds or when the standard logo would blend. Maintains full recognisability.
